AndyCApps — Privacy Policy & Support

Effective date: 30 September 2026 · Developer: AndyCApps, United Kingdom · Contact: andycapps1981@gmail.com

The short version: our games have no accounts and no sign-in. Your game progress stays on your device. We don’t collect, store or sell your personal information ourselves. The free versions show ads through Google AdMob, which uses your device’s advertising ID to serve and measure ads — you’ll be asked for consent where the law requires it. Details for each game are below.

Who we are

The games on this page are developed and published by AndyCApps, based in the United Kingdom. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at andycapps1981@gmail.com.

What our games store on your device

Each game saves your progress locally on your device only, so it works offline and remembers where you left off. Depending on the game this can include level and puzzle progress, scores, statistics and achievements, streaks, settings such as sound and theme, and a record of purchases you have made (so the game knows what you own).

This information never leaves your device, is not linked to your identity, and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free versions of our games show advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google and its advertising partners may collect information from your device, including:

  • Your device’s advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, operating system, app version)
  • Ad interaction data (which ads were shown, viewed or tapped)

We do not receive or store this information ourselves. You can read how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google’s privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices: in the UK, EEA and other regions where consent is required, the game shows a consent message letting you choose between personalised and non-personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time. On iPhone and iPad, games that use personalised ads ask for your permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt first. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Purchases

Optional purchases are processed entirely by the app store you downloaded the game from (Google Play Billing or Apple’s App Store). We never see or store your payment details. The only thing saved on your device is a local flag confirming what you own, and the store lets you restore purchases on a new device.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts, sign-ins, or collection of names, emails or contacts
  • No selling of personal information
  • No precise-location access, camera, microphone or contact permissions

Children

Our games are general-audience games and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Where consent applies, ad serving follows Google’s policies for the choice made on the consent form.

Coin Cosmos is the exception: it is made for players of all ages, with an age screen and non-personalised adverts for children and teens (see its section).

Data retention and deletion

Because game data lives only on your device, uninstalling the game deletes it. Advertising data collected by Google is retained and deleted according to Google’s retention policy; resetting your advertising ID unlinks previously collected ad data from your device identifier.

Your rights (UK GDPR)

Under UK data protection law you have rights over personal data, including access, correction and erasure. We hold no personal data about you on our systems; for data processed by Google for advertising, the controls above and Google’s privacy tools at myadcenter.google.com apply. You can also contact us at andycapps1981@gmail.com with any request or question, and you have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

Changes to this policy

If we change how a game handles data — for example by adding analytics or online features — we will update this page and the effective date above before the change ships.

One-tap orbit arcade game · Android and iOS

  • Ads: ads from Google AdMob, as described above. 🪙 Coin Cosmos (Android and iOS)

Coin Cosmos is made for players of all ages. When you first open it, it asks for your date of birth. Only an age band (child, teen or adult) is saved on your device. The date itself is not kept or sent. No adverts are shown until you have answered. You can enter your date of birth again in Stats, under Age and advert settings, but a later answer can only make adverts more restricted, never less. Your saved game stays on your device.

Adverts: the game shows adverts through Google AdMob. Children and teens see non-personalised adverts only. Adults in the UK, EU and similar regions are asked for consent through Google's consent form and can change that choice with the Privacy and ad choices button. Where consent is given or not required, adults may see personalised adverts. For children, Google states the advertising ID is not sent. AdMob automatically collects your IP address (to estimate general location), how you interact with the app, diagnostic information and device identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. More: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

On iPhone and iPad, adults are asked for App Tracking Transparency permission before any personalised adverts. Children and teens are not shown personalised adverts.

Purchases: optional permanent boosts are sold through Google Play, which handles payment. The game asks Google Play which boosts you own and keeps that on your device to unlock them.

The Android version has no accounts, no leaderboard and no chat, and sends no username or score anywhere.
