DreamTrail Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026
Developer: AndyCApps, United Kingdom
Contact: andycapps1981@gmail.com

This policy covers DreamTrail - Bedtime Stories on Android (com.andycapps.dreamtrail) and the DreamTrail web app.

Stories, accounts and advertising

DreamTrail offers illustrated choose-your-path bedtime stories, including reading groups for children. It has no account registration, sign-in, advertisements, chat or user-uploaded content. It does not ask for a child's name, date of birth, email address or contact details. The current release has no in-app purchases.

Information on your device

Your age-group selection and story choices are used within the app to show the story you choose. They are not submitted to a story database. The app may cache downloaded files on your device. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes that local cache. Story progress currently resets when you restart the app.

Online content and hosting

DreamTrail needs an internet connection. The app and its illustrations are served by Base44. Loading them sends the hosting service the technical information needed for a web request, including your IP address and browser or device information. Hosting services may keep technical server logs for operation and security. Base44's handling and retention of hosting information is described in Base44's privacy policy.

DreamTrail's optional Base44 app analytics and page-activity tracking were disabled on 12 October 2026. The current release does not send story choices, analytics heartbeat events or page-activity events. Earlier web visits may have generated Base44 analytics records; Base44 states that analytics data is automatically deleted after 60 days.

Purchases

If you buy DreamTrail through Google Play, Google handles the payment and purchase record. AndyCApps does not receive your card details. Google Play's own privacy and purchase terms apply.

Children

DreamTrail includes stories for ages 5-8, 9-11 and 12+. Children can read with a parent or carer without creating an account or providing personal details. We do not use advertising or behavioural profiling in DreamTrail.

Support and your rights

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. We use it to answer your request. Please do not send a child's personal details. To ask about access, correction or deletion of information, contact andycapps1981@gmail.com. UK users can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes

We will update this policy before introducing accounts, advertising, in-app purchases or new data handling.
