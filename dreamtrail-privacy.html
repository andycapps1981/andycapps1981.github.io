DreamTrail Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026
Developer: AndyCApps, United Kingdom
Contact: andycapps1981@gmail.com

This policy covers DreamTrail - Bedtime Stories on Android and iOS (com.andycapps.dreamtrail), and the separate DreamTrail web app.

Stories, accounts and advertising

DreamTrail offers illustrated choose-your-path bedtime stories, including reading groups for children. It has no account registration, sign-in, advertisements, chat or user-uploaded content. It does not ask for a child's name, date of birth, email address or contact details. The current release has no in-app purchases.

Information on your device

Your age-group selection and story choices are used within the app to show the story you choose. Sound and auto-play preferences are saved locally on your device. The sleep timer is used within the current session. Narration is pre-generated audio included with the app; listening does not send your voice or story choices to an AI service. They are not submitted to a story database. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stores on your device. Story progress currently resets when you restart the app.

Offline mobile apps and web hosting

The Android app (from version 1.0.7) and the iOS app work fully offline. Stories, illustrations and read-aloud narration are packaged inside the app. Story reading and playback make no external network requests and do not collect, transmit or share personal data or usage data. The Android app has no internet permission. On iOS, outside website links require a grown-up to pass a parental gate before opening in the system browser. If you choose to open an outside website, that website handles the resulting web request under its own privacy policy.

The DreamTrail web app is a separate version that needs an internet connection. It is served by Base44, which receives the technical information needed for a web request, including your IP address and browser information. Base44's handling of hosting information is described in Base44's privacy policy. DreamTrail's optional Base44 app analytics and page-activity tracking were disabled on 12 October 2026. Earlier web visits may have generated Base44 analytics records; Base44 states that analytics data is automatically deleted after 60 days.

Purchases

If you buy DreamTrail through Google Play or the Apple App Store, Google or Apple handles the payment and purchase record. AndyCApps does not receive your card details. The relevant app store's privacy and purchase terms apply.

Children

DreamTrail includes stories for ages 5-8, 9-11 and 12+. Children can read with a parent or carer without creating an account or providing personal details. We do not use advertising or behavioural profiling in DreamTrail.

Support and your rights

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. We use it to answer your request. Please do not send a child's personal details. To ask about access, correction or deletion of information, contact andycapps1981@gmail.com. UK users can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes

We will update this policy before introducing accounts, advertising, in-app purchases or new data handling.
